🔋 DJI Drone Battery Care

Safe Battery Handling

  • 🔋 Check the battery before every flight.
  • ⚡ Use the appropriate DJI charger and charging equipment.
  • 🌡️ Avoid charging or using the battery in extreme temperatures.
  • 💧 Keep the battery away from water and moisture.
  • 🔥 Do not use a battery that is swollen, damaged or leaking.
  • 📦 Store batteries in a cool, dry and safe place.
  • ✈️ Before flying, make sure the battery has sufficient charge for the planned flight.
  • ⚠️ Follow the battery safety instructions provided by DJI for your specific drone model.
