🌬️ Weather & Wind

☀️ Check the Weather

Always check the weather conditions before starting a drone flight. Weather can affect flight safety, visibility and battery performance.

💨 Wind Conditions

  • 💨 Check the expected wind conditions before flying.
  • 🚁 Strong wind can make the drone harder to control.
  • 🏠 Be careful around buildings and other areas where wind can change.
  • ⬇️ Avoid conditions that are beyond your drone's operating limits.

🌧️ Rain & Moisture

Avoid flying in rain or other conditions that could expose the drone to water unless the equipment is specifically designed and approved for those conditions.

🌡️ Temperature

Very hot or cold conditions can affect the drone and its battery. Follow the manufacturer's operating temperature guidance.

👀 Before Takeoff

  • ☀️ Check the weather.
  • 💨 Check wind conditions.
  • 🌧️ Check for rain or moisture.
  • 🔋 Check battery condition.
  • 🚁 Make sure the planned flight is suitable for the conditions.

⚠️ Safety First

If weather or wind conditions are unsuitable, do not fly. Always follow the manufacturer's instructions and applicable rules.
