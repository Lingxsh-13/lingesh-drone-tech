### DJI Avata 360

DJI Avata 360 is a 360-degree camera drone designed to capture immersive aerial videos.

Instead of recording only in one direction, a 360° camera can capture the surroundings, allowing you to choose the viewing angle later while editing.

🚁 Key Features

  • 🎥 360° panoramic video recording
  • 🚁 Compact and agile drone design
  • 🥽 FPV-style flying experience
  • 📱 Suitable for immersive content and social media
  • 🧠 Intelligent flight and stabilization features
  • 🎬 Allows creative camera-angle selection after recording
  • 🏔️ Useful for travel, adventure and outdoor filming

🎬 Uses

  1. Travel and adventure videos
  2. 360° aerial photography
  3. Vlogging
  4. Sports and action footage
  5. Cinematic videos
  6. Social-media content
