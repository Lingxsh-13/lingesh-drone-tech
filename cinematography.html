🎥 Drone Cinematography

🎬 What is Drone Cinematography?

Drone cinematography is the art of creating smooth and visually interesting aerial video using a drone.

🚁 Smooth Drone Movements

  • 🐢 Move the drone slowly and smoothly.
  • 🎯 Keep the subject in frame.
  • 📐 Avoid sudden movements.
  • 🔄 Practice controlled turns and camera movements.

🎯 Useful Shots

  • ⬆️ Reveal shot
  • ➡️ Tracking shot
  • 🔄 Orbit shot
  • ⬇️ Top-down shot
  • 🎬 Push-in and pull-out shots

☀️ Lighting

Good lighting can make aerial footage more attractive. Sunrise and sunset can provide interesting light and shadows.

🎞️ Planning the Shot

Before recording, think about the subject, flight path, camera direction and the final look you want to create.

⚠️ Safety First

Always maintain safe control of the drone and follow applicable rules and restrictions while recording aerial video.
