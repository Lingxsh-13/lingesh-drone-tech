🚁 Drone Flying Tips

🎮 Practice Controls

Practice basic drone controls in a safe and open area before attempting more complex movements.

🐢 Fly Smoothly

  • 🐢 Use gentle control inputs.
  • 🎯 Avoid sudden movements.
  • 📐 Keep the drone at a manageable distance.
  • 👀 Continuously monitor the drone and surroundings.

⬆️ Basic Movements

  • ⬆️ Ascend and descend smoothly.
  • ↔️ Practice forward and backward movement.
  • ↩️ Practice controlled turns.
  • 🔄 Practice simple orbit movements.

📍 Maintain Awareness

Keep track of the drone's position, surroundings and available battery throughout the flight.

🔋 Battery Planning

Start with enough battery for the planned flight and allow sufficient reserve to return and land safely.

⚠️ Safety First

Always maintain safe control of the drone and follow applicable aviation rules, restrictions and manufacturer instructions.
