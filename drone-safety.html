🛡️ Drone Safety

Safe Drone Flying

Always inspect your drone before every flight and make sure the battery, propellers and other equipment are in good condition.

Check the current airspace information and applicable restrictions before flying.

Maintain safe control of the drone and avoid flying in a way that could endanger people or property.

Follow the applicable DGCA rules and safety requirements whenever operating a drone.
