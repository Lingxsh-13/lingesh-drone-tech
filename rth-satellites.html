🛰️ RTH & Satellites

🚁 What is RTH?

RTH means Return To Home. It is a safety feature that can help the drone return toward its recorded Home Point when RTH is triggered.

🏠 Home Point

Before takeoff, make sure the Home Point has been recorded correctly. The Home Point is used as the reference location for the RTH function.

⚙️ How RTH Works

  • 📍 The drone records a Home Point.
  • 🚁 RTH can be triggered when required.
  • 🛰️ Positioning information helps determine the drone's location.
  • 🏠 The drone attempts to return toward the Home Point.
  • 👀 The pilot should continue monitoring the drone.

🛰️ Satellites & Positioning

DJI drones use satellite positioning systems to help determine their location and support flight functions.

📡 Why Satellite Positioning is Important

  • 🛰️ Helps the drone determine its position.
  • 📍 Supports location-related flight functions.
  • 🏠 Supports functions such as RTH.
  • ⚠️ Positioning warnings should be checked before flying.

⚠️ Before Takeoff

  • 🔋 Check the battery level.
  • 📍 Confirm the Home Point.
  • 🛰️ Check positioning status.
  • 🚁 Check the surrounding environment.
  • 👀 Keep the drone under your control.

🛡️ Important Safety Note

RTH is a safety feature and should not replace pilot awareness. Always follow the drone manufacturer's instructions and applicable aviation rules.
