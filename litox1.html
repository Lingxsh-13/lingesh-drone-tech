DJI Lito X1

The DJI Lito X1 is a compact sub-249g camera drone designed for aerial photography, cinematic videos and creative content.

It features a 1/1.3-inch CMOS sensor, f/1.7 aperture, omnidirectional obstacle sensing and forward-facing LiDAR for enhanced obstacle detection.

⚙️ Key Specs

Weight Approx. 249g
Camera 1/1.3-inch CMOS
Photo 48MP
Aperture f/1.7
Video 4K/60fps HDR
Slow Motion 4K/100fps
Flight Time Up to 36 min
Plus Battery Up to 52 min
Transmission DJI O4
Transmission Range Up to 15 km
Internal Storage 42GB
Obstacle Sensing Omnidirectional + Forward LiDAR
