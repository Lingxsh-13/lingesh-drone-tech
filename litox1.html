### DJI Lito X1

DJI Lito X1 is a lightweight camera drone designed for high-quality aerial photography, cinematic video and creative content production.

With a 1/1.3-inch CMOS sensor, f/1.7 aperture, intelligent flight features and advanced obstacle sensing, Lito X1 is designed to deliver high-quality footage while remaining portable.

⚙️ Key Specs

Weight Under 249g
Camera 1/1.3-inch CMOS
Aperture f/1.7
Photo Up to 48MP
HDR Video 4K / 60fps HDR
Slow Motion 4K / 100fps
Flight Time Up to 36 min
Battery Plus Up to 52 min
Transmission DJI O4 · Up to 15km*
Internal Storage 42GB
