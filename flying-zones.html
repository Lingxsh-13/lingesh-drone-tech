🗺️ Flying Zones

📍 Check the Area Before Flying

Before every flight, check the current airspace information and applicable restrictions for the location where you plan to fly.

🟢 Permitted Areas

Fly only where drone operations are permitted and where you have complied with the applicable requirements.

🔴 Restricted Areas

Some locations may have restrictions or prohibitions on drone operations. Do not fly in areas where your operation is not permitted.

🏢 Sensitive Locations

  • ✈️ Airports and aviation facilities
  • 🏛️ Government and other sensitive locations
  • 👥 Crowded public areas
  • 🚧 Areas with temporary flight restrictions

📱 Before Takeoff

  • 🗺️ Check the current airspace information.
  • 📍 Confirm the planned flight area.
  • ⚠️ Check applicable restrictions.
  • 🚁 Follow the required rules before flying.

🛡️ Safety First

Airspace restrictions can change. Always verify the current official information before each flight and follow applicable aviation rules.
