### DJI Neo 2

DJI Neo 2 is a compact and lightweight camera drone designed for easy aerial photography and video recording.

It is suitable for beginners as well as content creators.

🚁 Key Features

  • 📷 High-quality camera for photos and videos
  • 🚁 Small and lightweight design
  • 🎮 Easy controls for beginners
  • 👋 Hand/palm takeoff and landing features
  • 🧠 Intelligent flight modes and tracking features
  • 🎥 Useful for travel, vlogging, and social-media content
  • 🔋 Rechargeable intelligent flight battery
  • 📱 Can be controlled using compatible DJI devices/apps

🎬 Uses of DJI Neo 2

  1. Travel photography
  2. Vlogging
  3. Aerial video recording
  4. Landscape photography
  5. Sports and outdoor activities
  6. Social-media content creation
