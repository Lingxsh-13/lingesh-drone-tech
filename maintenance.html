🔧 DJI Drone Maintenance

Basic Drone Maintenance

  • 🔍 Inspect the drone before every flight.
  • 🛩️ Check the propellers for cracks, damage or deformation.
  • 🧹 Keep the camera, sensors and air vents clean.
  • 🔋 Check the battery condition before use.
  • 📦 Store the drone and accessories safely after flying.
  • ⚙️ Check that moving parts and equipment are functioning properly.
  • 💾 Keep the drone firmware and related software updated when appropriate.
  • ⚠️ If the drone is damaged or behaves abnormally, stop flying and follow the manufacturer's service guidance.
