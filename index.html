  • 🔵 Micro: > 250 grams to ≤ 2 kg
  • 🟡 Small: > 2 kg to ≤ 25 kg
  • 🟠 Medium: > 25 kg to ≤ 150 kg
  • 🔴 Large: > 150 kg
